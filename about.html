About Nauza Tu

A digital marketplace connecting vehicle sellers with people looking for vehicles in Kenya.

What Is Nauza Tu?

Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market is a digital vehicle marketplace designed to make it easier for people in Kenya to discover vehicles available for sale.

Instead of operating as a traditional motor dealership, Nauza Tu provides an online platform where vehicle owners, private sellers and eligible motor dealers can advertise vehicles and where potential buyers can browse listings and contact sellers directly.

Our goal is to bring vehicle sellers and potential buyers together through a simple, accessible and convenient online marketplace.

Our Role

Nauza Tu provides the digital platform through which vehicle sellers can showcase their vehicles.

Buyers can browse available listings, compare vehicles and contact the respective seller when they find a vehicle they are interested in.

The transaction and final agreement for the sale of a vehicle is between the buyer and the seller.

What We Do Not Do

Nauza Tu does not own the vehicles advertised on the marketplace.

We do not present marketplace vehicles as our own stock.

We do not provide vehicle financing as part of the marketplace.

We do not replace the buyer's responsibility to inspect and verify a vehicle before purchasing it.

2

3

Buyer Browses

4

Buyer Contacts Seller

The interested buyer contacts the seller directly to discuss the vehicle and arrange inspection or other next steps.

Important Buyer Notice

Nauza Tu is a marketplace and does not own the vehicles advertised by sellers.

Before purchasing any vehicle, buyers should independently verify the seller's identity, ownership documents, vehicle registration details, mileage, mechanical condition and any other relevant information.

Buyers should inspect the vehicle personally or through a qualified and trusted mechanic and should take appropriate precautions before making payment.

Do not rely solely on photographs or information contained in an online advertisement when deciding whether to purchase a vehicle.
