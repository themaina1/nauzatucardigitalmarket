About Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market

A Kenya-focused digital marketplace designed to make it easier for people to discover vehicles advertised by owners, dealers and other vehicle sellers.

Browse Vehicles Advertise Your Vehicle

A Marketplace Built Around Vehicle Discovery

Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market is an online vehicle marketplace connecting people looking to buy vehicles with people and businesses advertising vehicles for sale in Kenya.

Our role is to provide a convenient digital platform where vehicle advertisements can be discovered, compared and followed up through direct enquiries.

🔎

Discover

Browse vehicle advertisements by make, model, category, price, location and other available specifications.

⚖️

Compare

Review important vehicle specifications side by side to help you narrow down the vehicles that interest you.

📞

Connect

Once you find a vehicle that interests you, contact the advertiser directly where public contact details are available, or send an enquiry through Nauza Tu.

How Nauza Tu Works

A simple marketplace process for buyers and advertisers.

1

Browse

Search available vehicle advertisements and explore the vehicles that match your requirements.

2

Review

Look at the listed specifications, photographs, price, location and description.

3

Enquire

4

For Vehicle Buyers

Nauza Tu gives buyers a place to discover vehicles advertised in the Kenyan market.

  • Search and browse available vehicle advertisements.
  • Compare selected vehicles and specifications.
  • Review prices and vehicle locations.
  • Send enquiries about vehicles you are interested in.
  • Arrange viewing and inspection with the advertiser.
  • Carry out independent ownership and document checks.

For Vehicle Sellers

Owners, dealers and other legitimate vehicle advertisers can use Nauza Tu to reach potential buyers.

  • Submit a vehicle for advertising.
  • Provide accurate vehicle information.
  • Include photographs and a useful description.
  • Receive enquiries from interested buyers.
  • Communicate with potential buyers.
  • Complete the transaction directly with the buyer.

Important: Nauza Tu Is a Marketplace

Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market does not own the vehicles displayed on this website.

Nauza Tu is not the seller of the vehicles listed by advertisers. The vehicle owner, dealer or advertiser is responsible for the vehicle and the information supplied for the advertisement.

Buyers should independently verify the vehicle, its ownership, documentation, condition, history and any other relevant details before making a purchase or payment.

Nauza Tu does not guarantee that every advertised vehicle will remain available, that every advertised specification is accurate, or that a transaction between a buyer and advertiser will be completed.
