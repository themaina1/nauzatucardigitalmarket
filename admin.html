How to Publish an Approved Listing

  1. Receive the seller's advertisement information and photographs.
  2. Check the information supplied by the advertiser.
  3. Give the listing its next Nauza Tu ID, for example NT010.
  4. Upload the approved vehicle photographs into your repository's images folder.
  5. Enter the exact image filenames in this form.
  6. Click Generate Listing.
  7. Click Copy Listing.
  8. Open vehicles-data.js.
  9. Paste the generated listing before the final closing bracket of the vehicles array.
  10. Commit the change to GitHub.
  11. Open the Vehicles page and check that the listing appears correctly.
Remember: Do not publish a vehicle simply because someone submitted it. Review the advertisement first and only publish listings that meet your marketplace requirements.
