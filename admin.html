How to Add the Approved Vehicle

  1. Review the seller's submission and confirm that the listing has been approved.
  2. Make sure the vehicle photographs have been received and uploaded into your website's images folder.
  3. Enter the approved vehicle information above.
  4. Click Generate Listing Code.
  5. Click Copy Code.
  6. Open vehicles-data.js in your GitHub repository.
  7. Add the generated vehicle object before the final ];.
  8. Save and commit the change to GitHub.
  9. The approved vehicle will then appear on vehicles.html.
  10. The same vehicle will automatically become available on vehicle-details.html and compare.html, because those pages all use the central vehicle database.
