Before You Buy Any Car

NAUZA TU helps you discover and compare vehicles, but a recommendation is not a guarantee that a particular vehicle is mechanically sound or legally clear. Inspect the actual vehicle and verify the seller and documents.

✓ Inspect the vehicle physically
✓ Check the chassis/VIN
✓ Verify ownership documents
✓ Confirm the seller's identity
✓ Test drive before paying
✓ Use a trusted mechanic
