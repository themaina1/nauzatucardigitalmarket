Important Marketplace Notice

Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market is a digital marketplace and advertising platform. We connect potential buyers with vehicle sellers and advertisers.

Nauza Tu does not own the vehicles advertised on this website and is not the seller of those vehicles. Vehicle enquiries may be forwarded to or handled in connection with the respective advertiser.

Before purchasing any vehicle, buyers should independently verify ownership, documentation, vehicle condition, mileage, accident history, outstanding obligations and other relevant details. Never send payment solely because a vehicle appears on this website.
