Important About Nauza Tu

Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market is a digital marketplace. We do not own the vehicles advertised on this platform and we are not the seller of vehicles listed by vehicle owners or dealers.

Our role is to provide a platform where sellers can advertise vehicles and potential buyers can discover vehicles and contact sellers.

Buyers should independently inspect vehicles, verify ownership and documentation, confirm the vehicle's condition and verify all information provided by the seller before making any payment.
