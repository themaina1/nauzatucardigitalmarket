Privacy Policy

How Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market collects, uses and protects information submitted through our marketplace.

Important: Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market operates a digital vehicle marketplace. We may collect information submitted by buyers, sellers and advertisers in order to operate the website, process enquiries and manage vehicle advertisements.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market ("Nauza Tu", "we", "us" or "our") may collect, use, store and otherwise handle information provided through our website.

This policy applies to information submitted through our vehicle advertising forms, contact forms, enquiries and other interactions with the Nauza Tu website.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as:

  • Name
  • Telephone number
  • WhatsApp contact number
  • Email address
  • Location
  • Vehicle make and model
  • Vehicle year
  • Vehicle specifications
  • Vehicle mileage
  • Vehicle asking price
  • Vehicle photographs or other submitted material
  • Messages and enquiries
  • Information voluntarily provided when reporting a listing

3. Information Provided by Vehicle Advertisers

If you submit a vehicle for advertising, the information you provide may be used to create and manage a vehicle listing.

Some information may appear publicly on the website where necessary for the advertisement, while other information may remain available only to Nauza Tu for purposes such as handling buyer enquiries.

Advertisers should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information in vehicle descriptions, photographs or other listing material.

4. Information Provided by Buyers

If you contact Nauza Tu about a vehicle, submit a general enquiry, ask a question or report an advertisement, we may receive the contact and message information you provide.

This information may be used to respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, facilitate communication concerning the vehicle listing.

5. How We Use Information

Information received through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Communicate with vehicle advertisers.
  • Process and review vehicle advertising submissions.
  • Manage vehicle listings.
  • Respond to reports concerning listings.
  • Improve the marketplace and website.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Prevent or investigate misuse, fraud or unlawful activity.
  • Communicate important information concerning the platform.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

6. Vehicle Listings and Public Information

When an advertiser submits information for a vehicle listing, certain information may be displayed publicly to help potential buyers evaluate and enquire about the vehicle.

This may include information such as the vehicle's make, model, year, photographs, asking price, location, specifications and listing reference.

Advertisers should only submit information that they are comfortable having used for the purposes of advertising the vehicle.

7. Sharing Information

Nauza Tu does not sell personal information simply for the purpose of selling personal data.

Information may, however, be shared or disclosed where reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, respond to an enquiry, provide a requested service, protect the platform, comply with legal obligations or address suspected unlawful activity.

For example, where a buyer submits an enquiry about a particular vehicle, relevant enquiry information may need to be communicated to the relevant advertiser in order to facilitate the enquiry.

8. Third-Party Services

Nauza Tu may use third-party services to support website functionality, communications, hosting, forms, analytics or other technical operations.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Where the website links to an external service, users should review the privacy practices of that service before providing additional information.

9. Forms and Email

Information submitted through Nauza Tu contact and advertising forms may be transmitted to Nauza Tu through the form-processing services used by the website.

We may retain submitted enquiries and advertising information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage listings, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or comply with legal requirements.

10. Cookies and Technical Information

The website may use basic technical mechanisms such as cookies, browser storage or similar technologies where necessary to operate, secure or improve website functionality.

Your browser may also provide certain technical information automatically when you visit a website, such as device, browser or general usage information.

The exact technologies used may change as the website develops.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information handled through the website to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Retention of Information

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining marketplace records, managing advertisements, preventing abuse, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable Kenyan law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights relating to access, correction, objection, deletion or other lawful requests.

Requests concerning personal information may be submitted to Nauza Tu using the contact details provided below.

14. Accuracy of Information

Users are responsible for ensuring that information they submit to Nauza Tu is reasonably accurate and does not unlawfully contain another person's private information.

Advertisers should also ensure that they have appropriate authority before submitting another person's contact details, vehicle information or photographs.

15. Children's Information

The Nauza Tu marketplace is intended for people who are legally able to participate in vehicle-related enquiries and transactions. We do not knowingly seek unnecessary personal information from children.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the website, marketplace operations, technology or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page.

17. Kenyan Law

Nauza Tu operates in Kenya and intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Kenyan data protection and privacy requirements.

This policy should be read together with the Nauza Tu Terms & Conditions and other applicable notices published on the website.

18. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have a privacy question, wish to request access to information concerning you, believe your information has been incorrectly handled, or wish to raise a privacy concern, contact Nauza Tu.
