Privacy Policy

How Nauza Tu handles information submitted through the marketplace.

Important: Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market operates as a digital vehicle marketplace. We connect vehicle advertisers with potential buyers. We are not the owner or seller of the vehicles displayed on the platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as:

  • Name or business name.
  • Telephone number.
  • WhatsApp number.
  • Email address.
  • Vehicle location.
  • Vehicle make, model and specifications.
  • Vehicle asking price.
  • Vehicle description and photographs.
  • Information contained in enquiries sent to us.

2. How We Receive Information

Information may be submitted when you:

  • Submit a vehicle for advertising.
  • Contact Nauza Tu.
  • Make an enquiry about an advertised vehicle.
  • Communicate with us by email, telephone or WhatsApp.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process vehicle advertising submissions.
  • Review and manage marketplace listings.
  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Connect potential buyers with vehicle sellers.
  • Communicate with sellers about their listings.
  • Improve the website and marketplace experience.
  • Address suspected fraudulent or misleading listings.
  • Maintain records relating to marketplace enquiries.

4. Vehicle Seller Information

If you submit a vehicle for advertising, you understand that some information may be displayed publicly as part of the vehicle listing.

This may include the vehicle's make, model, year, location, asking price, specifications, photographs and other information relevant to the advertisement.

Seller contact information will only be displayed publicly where the listing arrangement permits it. Where direct seller contact is not published, potential buyers may be directed to contact Nauza Tu.

5. Information You Should Not Submit

Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information through the website. In particular, do not publicly upload or disclose:

  • Passwords.
  • PIN numbers.
  • Banking passwords.
  • Full payment card details.
  • National identification information unless specifically required through an appropriate process.
  • Original vehicle documents.

6. Vehicle Verification

Nauza Tu may review information supplied by sellers, but publication of a listing does not constitute a guarantee that the vehicle is genuine, legally owned, mechanically sound, accident-free, free from outstanding obligations or otherwise suitable for purchase.

Buyers are responsible for carrying out their own due diligence before entering into a transaction.

7. Sharing Information

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, respond to enquiries, communicate with sellers or buyers, deal with suspected fraud or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Nauza Tu does not represent that every third-party communication channel used by a seller is controlled by Nauza Tu.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services to process forms, host the website or provide communication functionality.

For example, seller and contact forms may be processed through an external form-processing service before being delivered to Nauza Tu.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Cookies and Website Data

The website may use basic browser technologies or third-party services that process technical information necessary to operate or improve the website.

Where applicable, users may control certain browser storage and cookie settings through their device or browser.

10. Data Security

Nauza Tu takes reasonable steps to protect information under its control. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for marketplace administration, communication, record keeping, dispute resolution, fraud prevention or legal compliance.
