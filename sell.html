Sell or Advertise Your Vehicle

List your car, SUV, pickup, van, truck or other vehicle on Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market and put it in front of potential buyers looking for vehicles in Kenya.

Important: Nauza Tu is a digital marketplace. Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market does not own the vehicles advertised on this website. Vehicles are offered by private sellers, vehicle owners and dealers. Your submission will be reviewed before it is published on the marketplace.

Vehicle Listing Application

Provide accurate information about the vehicle you want to advertise.

1. Seller Information

Tell us who is offering the vehicle.



2. Vehicle Information

Give potential buyers the basic specifications of the vehicle.

Please provide honest and accurate information. Avoid making claims that you cannot verify.



3. Vehicle Photos

Good photographs help potential buyers understand the vehicle.

For the best listing, prepare clear photographs showing: front, rear, both sides, interior, dashboard, seats, engine bay and any important defects.



4. Buyer Contact Preference

Tell us how you would like potential buyers to reach you.



5. Seller Confirmation

Please confirm the information you have submitted.
