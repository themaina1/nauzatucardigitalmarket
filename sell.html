Advertise Your Vehicle

Submit your vehicle to Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market and connect with potential buyers looking for vehicles in Kenya.

Important: Nauza Tu Digital Motor Market is a digital marketplace. We do not buy vehicles, own vehicles listed on this website, or act as the seller of advertised vehicles. Your submission is reviewed before publication. Submitting a vehicle does not automatically guarantee that the advertisement will be published.

Before You Submit

Please provide accurate information about the vehicle and make sure you are the owner or are authorized by the owner to advertise it.

1. Seller / Advertiser Information

Tell us who is submitting the vehicle advertisement.

If different from your phone number.

2. Vehicle Information

Provide as much accurate information as possible so that potential buyers can understand the vehicle being advertised.

Please do not hide known defects or important information about the vehicle.

3. Vehicle Photos

Good photographs help potential buyers understand the vehicle before arranging a viewing.

Recommended Photos

  • Front exterior
  • Rear exterior
  • Both sides of the vehicle
  • Interior and seats
  • Dashboard and instrument cluster
  • Engine bay
  • Tyres and wheels
  • Any visible damage or defects
  • Other important features

Do not send misleading, heavily edited or unrelated photographs.

The current Nauza Tu website submission form does not directly upload image files. After submitting the form, we can arrange for photographs to be sent by WhatsApp or email.

4. Seller Authorization & Confirmation

Please read each statement carefully before submitting your vehicle for advertising.

Public Seller Contact

By default, your personal phone and WhatsApp numbers will not be displayed publicly on the vehicle listing. Interested buyers can contact Nauza Tu about the listing.
