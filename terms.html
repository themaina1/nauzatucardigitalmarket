Anyone submitting a vehicle for advertising agrees that:

  • They should disclose material information that a reasonable buyer would need to know.
  • They should not use the marketplace for fraudulent activity.
  • They should respond honestly to potential buyers.

Buyers are responsible for conducting their own due diligence before purchasing a vehicle.

A buyer should independently consider checking:

  • Mileage.
  • Accident history.
  • Service history.
  • Physical condition.
  • Any other matter relevant to the purchase.

Where appropriate, buyers should use qualified mechanics, relevant authorities and professional advisers before completing a transaction.
